Fiduciary duties when a growth company misses its plan

Boards rarely face a fiduciary question when the plan is working. The record matters when forecasts turn, liquidity tightens and directors must choose between competing stakeholders.

A board is not required to be prescient. It is required to be informed, disinterested and deliberate. Those qualities are demonstrated less by the final decision than by the process that produced it: the information requested, the alternatives considered, the advice received and the minutes that record the discussion.

Start with the record, not the slogan

When a company misses a forecast, the first task is factual. Which assumptions failed, when did management know, what obligations mature next, and which constituencies have enforceable rights? A concise chronology and a matrix of contractual constraints will often narrow the board's real options faster than a general discussion of duties.

Good minutes do not merely state that directors discussed a matter. They show what was before the board, who advised it and which alternatives were rejected.

Conflicts should be identified early

Directors affiliated with a lender, investor or counterparty may need separate counsel, a recusal or a carefully defined committee. The applicable standard of review can turn on whether a majority of decision-makers was disinterested and independent. That determination should not be reconstructed after a transaction is challenged.

A practical checklist

  • Prepare a dated chronology of forecast changes and board communications.
  • Identify contractual thresholds, liquidity covenants and notice obligations.
  • Confirm which directors have interests in the proposed transaction.
  • Engage independent advisers where the transaction or conflict structure requires it.
  • Record the alternatives, the advice and the reasons for the chosen course.

The point is not to create defensive paperwork. It is to give directors the information and structure needed to make a difficult decision well. A disciplined record is a by-product of that work.
