About the firm

A traditional standard, practiced with modern discipline.

Founded in New York in 1998, Whitmore & Hale LLP advises companies, boards, investors and families where the legal record must be as strong as the commercial judgment behind it.

Our teams are deliberately small and senior-led. We do not measure service by volume of activity. We measure it by whether the client understands the decision in front of them, the evidence supporting it, and the cost of getting it wrong.

FAQ

Common questions, plain answers.

A few things clients ask before an engagement begins.

We generally work under hourly rates, fixed-fee phases or alternative arrangements depending on the matter. Rates are on request and are confirmed in a written engagement letter before work begins.
We offer a preliminary discussion at no charge to understand the matter, identify conflicts and determine whether the firm is the right fit. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.
Our attorneys are admitted in New York and Massachusetts, with additional federal and appellate admissions listed on each attorney profile. We also coordinate local counsel for cross-border matters.
Bring the operative contracts, correspondence, court papers and a short timeline. A concise chronology is often more useful than an unfiltered document dump.
A conflicts check is completed before substantive discussions. We use a centralized matter-opening process and will decline or seek a waiver where required by the applicable rules of professional conduct.
No. We can explain the law, the evidence and the likely range of outcomes, but past results and published case figures do not guarantee a similar outcome in another matter.
