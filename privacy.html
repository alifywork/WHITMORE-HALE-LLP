Scope

This page describes the informational website concept for Whitmore & Hale LLP. It is not a client portal, does not authenticate users and does not send form contents to a server. A production law-firm website would require a reviewed policy that reflects its actual intake, analytics and document systems.

Information we collect

The site does not run third-party analytics, advertising pixels or remote font requests. All fonts, icons, scripts, logos and images are loaded from this site's own assets/ directory. Standard web-server logs may record an IP address, requested path, user agent and timestamp for security and diagnostics.

Local storage

The EN / ES language control stores a single preference, wh-language, in your browser's local storage. It contains either en or es and is not transmitted to the firm. Clearing browser data removes it.

Forms and the client portal

Consultation, newsletter, careers and portal forms are front-end demonstrations. They validate locally, display a confirmation and then reset. No credentials, messages or email addresses are transmitted. Do not enter real passwords or confidential facts into the portal preview.

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy may be directed to privacy@whitmore-hale.com or +1 (212) 555-0170. This notice may be updated as the site's functionality changes.
