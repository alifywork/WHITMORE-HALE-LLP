About the Firm

A New York firm built around partner accountability, careful records and long client relationships since 1998.

Our history

Founded on a simple professional premise.

The client should know who is responsible for the advice, what the work will cost and which facts matter most.

Whitmore & Hale began in 1998 with four lawyers and two practice groups. Growth has been deliberate: each new capability answers a recurring client need rather than a market trend. The Boston office, regulatory group and pro bono clinic all followed that pattern.

Today, forty attorneys work from Midtown Manhattan and Boston's Financial District. Matter teams remain intentionally compact, with a named partner accountable from engagement through resolution.

New York · A standard of careful work
Values

Three principles that govern the work.

They shape staffing, budgets, writing and the advice we are willing to give.

Integrity

We say no to a position the record cannot support. Conflicts are resolved before advice is given.

Senior attention

Partners stay close to the evidence, the drafting and the client conversation.

Proportion

The legal response is sized to the actual risk, not to the volume of work that could be billed.

Firm timeline

Steady growth, no reinventions.

A chronological view of the decisions that shaped the firm.

1998

Founded in New York

Arthur Hale and Eleanor Whitmore open the firm with four lawyers and a corporate-litigation practice.

2004

Boston office

A Massachusetts desk is established to serve New England financial and family-owned businesses.

2011

Regulatory group

The firm adds banking and investigations capability as clients' supervisory demands increase.

2017

Cross-border desk

A vetted international counsel network is formalized for inbound and outbound transactions.

2022

Pro bono clinic

A monthly New York community clinic becomes a firm-wide program.

2026

Twenty-eight years

Forty attorneys across two offices, still organized around partner accountability.
